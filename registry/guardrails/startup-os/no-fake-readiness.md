# Guardrail: No Fake Readiness

## Rule

Do not label a prototype, experiment, or release as ready without naming missing checks, shortcuts, and known issues.

## Applies to

- MVP launches
- weekly releases
- demos presented as production systems

## Enforcement

1. Record known issues and skipped checks explicitly.
2. Distinguish prototype, beta, and production language.
3. Prefer a blunt readiness summary over reassuring but vague language.
