# Guardrail: Human Approval Required

## Rule

Do not claim or imply final approval in legal, financial, compliance, privacy, security, or material spending decisions.

## Applies to

- contracts and policy interpretation
- pricing and financial commitments
- privacy or regulatory changes
- outbound claims that could create liability

## Enforcement

1. Restrict agent outputs to prechecks, issue lists, and escalation recommendations.
2. Require explicit human signoff before final action in these domains.
3. Surface unknowns instead of filling them with confident prose.
